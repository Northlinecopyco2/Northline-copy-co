A quick example

From generic to customer-focused.

A sample rewrite for a fictional home-cleaning business. It shows the approach — not a client result or testimonial.

Before · Generic

Welcome to our cleaning company.

We provide professional cleaning services at affordable prices. Contact us today to learn more about our services.

After · Customer-focused

Come home to clean — without losing your weekend.

From regular upkeep to a one-time deep clean, get a home that feels refreshed and a schedule that works for you. Tell us what you need and request a quote.
